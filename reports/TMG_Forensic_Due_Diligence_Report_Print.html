1.1 Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) & Capital Structure

Talaat Moustafa Group Holding S.A.E. is incorporated under Egyptian Law No. 8 of 1997 and listed on the Egyptian Exchange under the ticker TMGH.CA. The paid-in equity base is established at EGP 20.635 Billion divided into 2,063,560,000 ordinary shares at par value EGP 10.00.

The shareholding structure represents a strategic coalition of founding family control and sovereign institutional capital:

  • Talaat Moustafa Family & Affiliates (Alexandria for Real Estate Investment): ~43.2% controlling voting block, exercising centralized executive leadership through Hesham Talaat Moustafa (CEO & Managing Director).
  • ADQ Sovereign Wealth Fund (Abu Dhabi Developmental Holding Company via ICON Hotels): Landmark $800 Million equity investment acquiring 40.5% of TMG's hospitality platform, providing critical hard-currency backing and expanding non-controlling equity reserves to EGP 33.5 Billion in FY2024 and EGP 59.5 Billion in FY2025.
  • Free Float & Institutional Investors (EGX & International GDRs): ~25.5% circulating among global and domestic asset managers.

1.2 The Contractual Privity Firewall Architecture

A paramount finding of this technical audit is the systematic legal ring-fencing separating asset-owning entities from operational liability:

  1. Asset Holding SPVs: Master land titles are held strictly in asset-owning subsidiaries (e.g., Arab Company for Projects and Urban Development for Madinaty/Noor; Mediterranean Urban Development Company for SouthMed).
  2. General Contracting Intermediaries: Landholding entities never contract directly with specialized trade vendors. Instead, they execute internal Turnkey / EPC agreements with captive general contractors: Alexandria Construction Company (ACC) or Atrium Quality Contractors.
  3. Subcontractor Privity Decoupling: Trade contractors (MEP, earthworks, water treatment, lagoons, structural concrete) enter into agreements exclusively with ACC or Atrium. Under Articles 145 and 152 of the Egyptian Civil Code, contractual rights and obligations are restricted solely to the immediate signing parties.
  4. Forensic Consequence: In the event of default, payment disputes, or inflation claims, subcontractors cannot place liens against TMG landbank titles or freeze parent bank accounts. Claimants are confined to arbitration against ACC under CRCICA rules, requiring heavy upfront filing fees and multi-year proceedings.
