Core Investigative Syntheses & Technical Findings

1. The Captive EPC Model & Contractual Tier Shielding

TMG Holding does not execute whole-project construction contracts directly with independent general contractors (e.g., Orascom, Arab Contractors, or Hassan Allam). Instead, works are funnelled through its captive contractor, Alexandria Construction Company SAE (ACC), or specialized turnkey vehicles like Atrium Quality Contractors.

This structure legally insulates TMG Holding:

  • Trade subcontractors in electrical, mechanical, plumbing, and structural trades hold privity strictly with ACC or Tier-1 contractors, preventing direct enforcement or bank account garnishments against TMG Holding or Arab Company for Projects & Urban Development.
  • Parent guarantee instruments are typically absent; contractual liability terminates at the subsidiary level.

2. The Interim Payment Certificate (المستخلصات) Gatekeeping Bottleneck

Consolidated financial notes disclose that project construction costs are recognized and capitalized strictly against approved payment certificates (*مستخلصات معتمدة*). Field investigation confirms dedicated technical teams at Madinaty Headquarters review and audit incoming contractor logs before financial signoff.

  • Measurement Re-audits: Routine disputes over site quantities and variations defer certificate approval without putting the developer in legal default under Egyptian Civil Code rules.
  • Commercial Paper Settlement: Upon approval, payments are frequently issued via commercial notes payable (*أوراق دفع*) with tenors stretching 90 to 270 days, transferring working capital costs downstream.

Actionable Due Diligence Protocols for Prospective Subcontractors

  • Privity Check: Confirm whether the contract is signed by TMG Holding or an operational subsidiary. Trade packages signed with Alexandria Construction Company cannot enforce claims against TMG Holding.
  • Refuse Pure Pay-When-Paid Terms: Require milestone payment windows rather than terms tied entirely to upstream developer disbursements.
  • Commercial Notes Ceilings: Establish clear limits on post-dated commercial paper (*أوراق دفع*), with maturities capped at 60 to 90 days.
  • Material Escalation Clauses: Tie input cost adjustments directly to Ministry of Housing price bulletins to protect against currency devaluations.
