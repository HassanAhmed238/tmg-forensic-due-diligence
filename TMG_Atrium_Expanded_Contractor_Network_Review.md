# Expanded TMG / Atrium Contractor Network Review

**Cut-off:** 12 October 2026

This standalone addendum identifies publicly documented main contractors, joint ventures, subcontractors and suppliers that have worked on TMG/Atrium projects, and separates payment allegations from project-participation announcements. It is not a complete vendor register; most subcontracts and purchase orders are not publicly disclosed.

## Main result

The network review identifies, among others, CCC Electromechanical / CCC in joint venture with Atrium; Concrete Plus on SouthMED; Everlast Construction at Open Air Mall Madinaty; Devzah SI in a Four Seasons Cairo Capital CP05 material record; Knauf Egypt at Four Seasons Luxor; EMAS El Sewedy at Noor City; and Siraj Lighting at East Hub. Project-management/consultancy entities named in the public chain include Hill International, Shaker Consultancy Group, Mimar Interiors, Hamza Associates, Dar El Handasah/EHAF and ECG. Their roles are not the same as a contractor's payment obligation.

The clearest direct published contractor-dues allegation remains the named 2008 Madinaty complaint against Alexandria Construction Company. A 2024 Mada Masr report describes non-payment of local subcontracting work linked by an anonymous source to upstream payment delays by the Armed Forces to SouthMED prime contractors; it does not prove that TMG or Concrete Plus directly withheld the money. No recent, independently corroborated public case was found that identifies AQC, the Atrium–CCC JV or the other named project companies as direct debtors for a specific overdue subcontractor certificate.

## Sentiment classification

- **Direct negative contractor-payment evidence:** 2008 ACC/Madinaty complaint, with company-side response in the press.
- **Indirect chain-of-payment concern:** 2024 SouthMED allegation; upstream payer attribution to Armed Forces in report, no direct attribution to TMG/Concrete Plus.
- **Positive but limited signals:** Everlast project participation, Knauf technical collaboration post, EMAS/Siraj supply/project posts, Concrete Plus repeat contracts, and a former Atrium QS profile describing invoice processing. None proves timely payment.
- **No proven conclusion:** current frequency of delayed IPCs, retention disputes, deductions or final-account withholding at TMG/AQC.

## Named parties recommended for direct reference checks

CCC / CCC Electromechanical, Concrete Plus, Everlast Construction, Knauf Egypt, Devzah SI, Siraj Lighting, EMAS El Sewedy, Alexandria Construction Company, Arab Company for Projects and Urban Development, Arab Company for Urban Investment, plus the relevant project commercial managers and certifiers. For each, confirm the exact contract entity and request (with consent) redacted IPC submission/approval/due/paid dates, retention status, variations, disputed deductions and final-account close-out.
